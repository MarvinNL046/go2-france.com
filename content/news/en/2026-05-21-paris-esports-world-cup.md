# Paris to Host Esports World Cup 2026 with Record $75M Prize Pool

Paris has been selected to host the **Esports World Cup 2026**, marking a historic moment for competitive gaming in France. The event will run from **July 6 to August 23, 2026**, bringing together more than 2,000 players from over 200 clubs across 100+ countries to compete in 24 games across 25 tournaments. The prize pool will exceed **$75 million**, making it one of the most lucrative esports competitions ever held.

This represents the first time the Esports World Cup will be held outside Saudi Arabia, where the inaugural event established the competition as a global phenomenon. According to the [Esports World Cup](https://www.esportsworldcup.com/en/news/ewc26-to-be-hosted-in-paris-france), the decision to relocate to Paris reflects the organization's long-term vision of rotating the event through major cities worldwide while maintaining stability for fans, players, and teams.

## Why Paris?

The Esports Foundation selected Paris following an extended evaluation process, citing the city's proven track record hosting world-class sporting events and its status as a global capital of sport, culture, and entertainment. Ralf Reichert, CEO of the Esports Foundation, emphasized that while Riyadh "helped turn the Esports World Cup into a global phenomenon," Paris offers the international accessibility, infrastructure, and competitive conditions necessary to deliver the event at full global scale.

The decision also reflects strong local support and a passionate French gaming community. The organizers noted that the move was made "in light of the current regional situation," providing clarity and stability for attendees planning their travel and event participation.

Importantly, the relocation is not permanent. Riyadh remains central to the event's long-term future, with plans to return in 2027, establishing a sustainable international rotation model.

## What to Expect in Paris

The Paris edition will feature the same competitive lineup as originally planned, with no changes to the games included in the Club Championship. However, there may be slight adjustments to the tournament schedule, with full details to be announced in the coming weeks.

The event will span seven weeks of elite competition, showcasing the world's top esports talent across multiple titles. The scale of the operation—involving thousands of players, hundreds of clubs, and fans from every continent—underscores Paris's capacity to host such a massive international gathering.

Beyond the competitions themselves, organizers have promised an enhanced fan experience through the **EWC Festival**, though specific details about this component will be shared later.

## What's Next for Visitors

Tickets for EWC26 in Paris will go on sale in the coming weeks. Those who purchased tickets for the originally planned Riyadh edition will receive full refunds processed to their original payment method within 21 working days. Early ticket holders will receive a special promotional code when the Paris ticketing platform opens.

The specific venue in Paris will be announced shortly, along with the complete tournament schedule and fan experience details. The Esports Foundation is working to finalize logistics to ensure a seamless experience for the global community attending.

For travelers planning to visit [Paris](https://go2-france.com/city/paris/) during the event, the seven-week window from early July through late August offers an excellent opportunity to experience the city's cultural attractions alongside world-class esports competition. More information about accommodation, transportation, and venue details will become available as the event approaches.

The relocation of the Esports World Cup to Paris signals growing recognition of France's importance in the global gaming ecosystem and reinforces the country's position as a premier destination for international sporting and cultural events.
